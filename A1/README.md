# A1 — Working with Open Data

**Dataset:** Burke-Gilman Trail counter north of NE 70th St
**Source:** https://data.seattle.gov/resource/2z5v-ecg8 (pulled 2026-20-06)
**License / terms of use:** Public Domain

## Questions I asked
1. When is the trail busiest, and when is it quietest? We defined busy within a single hour combining bikes and pedestrians together.
2. Do cyclists and pedestrians use the trail at different times of day? If they differ, what might explain it?
3. Is there evidence people use the Burke-Gilman to commute, not just for fun? We compared weekdays vs. weekends looking at the averge of bikers and pedestrians combined by hour of day. We defined a communting pattern as having peak usage in the morning and after work (around 5:00) during weekdays.
4. The counter records north vs. south separately. Are mornings mostly one direction and evenings the other? What does this tell us about who's using the trail and where they're heading?
